Legal Disclaimer & Privacy Architecture

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee: IPTV Dekh enforces strict client-side data sovereignty. No user-inputted URLs, custom M3U playlist assets, stream histories, or MAC addresses are ever sent to, recorded by, or cached on our backend servers. All configuration state resides strictly in your browser's private localStorage sandbox.

Client-Side Player Architecture

IPTV Dekh operates solely as an in-browser media interface. Stream decoding occurs via HTML5 & Hls.js connecting directly to public content distribution networks.

FAST Channels & Third-Party Endpoints

Samsung TV Plus, LG Channels, and public FAST feeds are distributed free-to-air by respective networks. Stream availability is subject to third-party endpoints and ISP routing.
