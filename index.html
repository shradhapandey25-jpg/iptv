Legal Disclaimer & DMCA Policy

Non-Hosting & Aggregation Policy: IPTV Dekh does not host, upload, store, archive, transmit, or re-broadcast any video, audio, or multimedia content on its servers. All streams and playlists accessible via this application are indexed from publicly available Internet links and third-party media servers.

1. Client-Side Player Architecture

IPTV Dekh operates solely as a client-side media player interface. All video streaming and decoding take place directly within the end user's web browser connecting straight to third-party CDNs. None of the video data routes through our hosting infrastructure.

2. Location & Bandwidth Constraints

Stream availability is subject to third-party broadcasting endpoints, regional broadcast licenses, internet service provider routing, and local network speeds. IPTV Dekh cannot guarantee uptime or continuous latency-free broadcast from external endpoints.

3. Copyright & Intellectual Property

All trademarks, channel names, logos, and media streams are the intellectual property of their respective owners. IPTV Dekh is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any of the broadcast networks displayed.

4. DMCA & Content Removal

If you are a copyright owner or an agent thereof and believe that any stream link indexed on this service infringes upon your copyright, please contact the third-party web host or original CDN broadcasting the stream.
